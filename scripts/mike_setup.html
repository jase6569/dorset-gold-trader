Start here: set up your app

Do these once, in order, on your iPhone. It takes about 15 minutes. Tick each step as you finish it and the app keeps track for you.

Before you start: ask Jase for your alert code. It starts dg- followed by 24 letters and numbers. It's private, so it's never shown in the app. Keep it somewhere safe, like a note on your phone.

  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. From now on, open the app from the Dorset Gold icon on your home screen.
  1. Install the free ntfy app: get it from the App Store.
  2. Open ntfy. When it asks to send you notifications, tap Allow. This matters: without it the alerts arrive silently.
  3. Tap + at the top right.
  4. Type your alert code exactly, starting dg-. Leave "Use another server" switched off.
  5. Tap Subscribe.
  1. Open your iPhone's Settings, scroll down to Notifications, then tap ntfy.
  2. Check Allow Notifications is on, and Lock Screen, Notification Centre and Banners are all ticked.
  3. Turn on Time Sensitive Notifications if you see it, so important alerts get through.
  4. If you use Focus or Do Not Disturb, add ntfy to the allowed apps.

Type your alert code and tap the button. A test message should pop up on your phone within a few seconds. If it doesn't, go back over steps 2 and 3.

  1. Tap My gold in the bottom bar, then the Make money tab.
  2. Scroll to Lot alerts, type the same alert code and tap Turn on lot alerts.
  3. You'll then get a ping when a batch of gold should go to the refiner, plus a summary of your week every Sunday evening. They arrive in the same ntfy alerts.
  1. In My gold, open the Send tab. Under Buying in, enter roughly how many grams you buy in a month, and the most stock you want to hold. Start with 2 months.
  2. Open the My stock tab and add the gold you're holding now: weight, carat, what you paid and the date. Gold you buy through the Buy log from now on is added for you.
  3. Open the Prices tab. It uses Mastermelt's trade counter price unless you change it. If you get a different rate, choose My own % and type it in.
  4. Open Make money and set the margin you want to keep (for example 20%). It then shows the most to pay per gram each day.
  1. Tap Buy log in the bottom bar, then Settings.
  2. Fill in your business details: business name, address, phone (01202 117896), your name as the staff member and your usual location. Tap Save settings.
  3. Set a PIN. The Buy log holds customers' ID photos, so it needs locking.
Open the Buy log
  1. On your phone, search Google for Dorset Gold & Jewellery while signed in to the Google account that manages your business listing.
  2. On your business panel, tap Ask for reviews (or Get more reviews) and copy the link. It usually starts https://g.page/r/
  3. In the Buy log, open Settings, find Google reviews, paste the link and tap Save.
  4. Tap Show my QR code and scan it with another phone to check it opens your review page.

After each purchase, the record shows a QR code for the customer to scan, or you can ask by text, WhatsApp or email.

  1. In the Buy log, open Settings and tap Back up now.
  2. Choose Google Drive (or email it to yourself).
  3. Do this every week. Everything lives only on this phone, so if it's lost or reset, the backup is the only copy. The app reminds you after 7 days.
What happens next

You don't need to keep the app open. It checks gold every hour, day and night, and messages you when the call on your stock changes, plus a summary every Friday evening. Days with no message are normal. If even the Friday summary doesn't arrive, check step 3 again. The rest of this guide explains every page in detail.
