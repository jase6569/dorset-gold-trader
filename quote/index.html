What's your gold worth? Send us a photo.

Take a few photos of your gold jewellery, coins or broken bits and we'll give you a rough price in about a minute. It's free and there's no obligation to sell.

Step 1

Your photos

  • Lay everything flat on a plain surface, ideally near a window.
  • Get close enough to see any hallmarks, the tiny stamped numbers like 375, 750 or 916.
  • A coin or your hand in the shot helps us judge the size.

Step 2

Happy with that?

Leave your number and we'll call you to book a free home visit. We test and weigh everything in front of you at your kitchen table, and you decide there and then whether to sell.

Your photos, quote and details go to Dorset Gold & Jewellery so we can call you back. We don't use them for anything else. See our privacy policy.

Quotes use today's trade gold price and are a guide only. The final price depends on testing and weighing your items at your visit. You'll need photo ID to sell, because the law asks us to record it for every purchase.
